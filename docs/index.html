AppPilot

A calm Android home screen. Your most-used apps sit in one plain list, and the list magnifies under your thumb as you slide down it.

Android 8.0 or newer Free · no ads · no account Nothing leaves your phone

Open the link on your Android phone. It isn't on the Play Store yet, so your phone will ask before installing it. The steps are below.

What it does

A list that learns

Apps are ordered by how often you open them, or by screen time, over the last day up to the last month. Pin favourites to the top, and hide the ones you never use.

Made for one thumb

Move the list up, down or to the left side. Change the text size, how much it magnifies and how many apps show.

Shortcuts in the list

Add a favourite chat, a web page or "directions home" straight to the list, from an app's menu or "Add to home screen".

Gestures

Swipe up for all apps with search (apps and contacts), down for the notification shade, and set swipes and double-tap to open what you like.

Your colours

Eight colour schemes, or pick every colour yourself. Show your wallpaper behind either side.

Private and accessible

No account, no ads, no internet use. Works with TalkBack, and settings can be backed up to a file.

Install it (about 2 minutes)

1 · Install

  1. Tap Download AppPilot.apk on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway. It warns because the app isn't from the Play Store yet.

2 · Set up

  1. Open AppPilot and tap Make AppPilot your home screen.
  2. Tap Allow usage access so it can rank your apps.
  3. For notifications, tap the prompt on the home screen. If the switch is greyed out: App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then try again.

Going back

To return to your usual home screen at any time: Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app, and pick your old one. Nothing is lost.

To update later, download the link again and install it over the top. Your settings are kept.
